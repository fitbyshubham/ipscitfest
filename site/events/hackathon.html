The Doon School · 08–10 December 2026

Hackathon

Schools are dissolved into companies. Each company elects a CEO, CTO and CMO after the Opening Ceremony, and only then is the problem statement released.

Cross-school venture build

You will not be competing with your own school.

Rules

How it runs

  1. Participants transform conceptual ideas into market-ready business solutions, combining technical expertise with entrepreneurial thinking.
  2. The event forms corporate entities (“companies”) spanning multiple schools. For example, five companies may be established, each comprising five participating schools (15 members per company). Final numbers depend on total registrations and will be communicated to registered schools in September.
  3. After the Opening Ceremony, companies appoint a CEO, CTO and CMO to oversee task delegation, steer strategic choices and lead the company.
  4. The problem statement is released only after leadership selection. Companies then work until the final hour before the Closing Ceremony.
  5. Deliverables: a functional prototype (hardware, software or both), a viable business and marketing strategy, and a final pitch presentation.
  6. Personal hardware (laptops) is required. AI assistance is permitted for development and design, but teams must demonstrate a profound understanding of their entire output.

Fabrication. For hardware projects, participants have access to campus fabrication resources, including 3D printing facilities.
